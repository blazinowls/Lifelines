Lifelines Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy explains how the Lifelines app ("the app"), published by Collin Giusti, handles information.

Information we collect

We do not collect your name, email address, or any account information. The app has no accounts. Your game progress, achievements, and settings are saved only on your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and information about how you interact with ads in order to show and measure ads. Players in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. See Google's policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data deletion

Because your game data is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time by using "Reset all saved data" in the app's Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, other than the advertising provider described above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page.
